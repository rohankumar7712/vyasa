FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for the online Parent Portal?

New parents will receive an automated activation email with credential guidelines at the start of the academic year. If you have not received your setup link, please contact our IT concierge helpdesk.

What is the procedure for scheduling a PTM meeting?

Parent-Teacher Meetings are scheduled through the Parent Portal app. Go to the 'PTM Scheduler' tab, select your child's class teacher, and book from the available 15-minute consultation slots.

Where can I purchase the prescribed school uniforms and books?

Prescribed textbooks and school uniforms can be purchased directly from our authorized partner store located at the VIS Main Administration Block, open Monday to Saturday from 9:30 AM to 3:30 PM.

How does the school manage student transport safety?

Every school bus is equipped with a live GPS tracking beacon accessible on the Portal, dynamic speed governors, and a verified school security steward on every route.

What is the school policy on child sickness and leave?

If your child is sick, please update an absence notification directly via the Parent Portal before 8:30 AM. A formal medical certificate must be attached for leaves exceeding three consecutive school days.
