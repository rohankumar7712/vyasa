STEP-BY-STEP FLOW

Our Enrollment Roadmap

We have streamlined our intake journey into six simple milestones to ensure clarity and support at each checkpoint.

STAGE 01 1

Online Inquiry

Submit the quick digital inquiry form with student details and interest stream.

STAGE 02 2

Campus Interaction

Experience our classrooms and meet counselors to align educational goals.

STAGE 03 3

Registration

Submit the formal application form along with the necessary prior academic records.

STAGE 04 4

Assessment

A soft diagnostic appraisal evaluating conceptual and language readiness.

STAGE 05 5

Counseling Interview

A gentle meeting with the Principal to finalize stream matches and options.

STAGE 06 6

Offer & Enrollment

Receive the formal admission offer letter and secure the seat with fees.

AGE & CRITERIA

Grade-Wise Eligibility Guidelines

Please review our standard age limits calculated as of June 1st of the entry academic year, aligned with central board mandates.

Grade Level Min Age Requirement Prerequisites & Prior Qualifications
Kindergarten (Nursery / LKG) 3 Years + as of June 1st N/A - Observational readiness interaction only
Primary School (Grades 1 - 5) 6 Years + for Grade 1 Promotional card from accredited nursery program
Middle School (Grades 6 - 8) 11 Years + Report card showing successful promotion from grade prior
High School (Grades 9 - 10) 14 Years + CBSE aligned prior grade clearance certificate
Pre-University (Grades 11 - 12) 16 Years + Class 10 board mark sheet with minimum 75% score
