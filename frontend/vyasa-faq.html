PRIMARY CATEGORY

General Campus Queries

Understand our foundational structure, Bengaluru campus location, and primary core values.

What is the Vyasa Group of Institutions?
Vyasa Group of Institutions is a premier educational establishment dedicated to academic excellence, innovative pedagogy, and sound character development. We provide global standard pedagogy combined with specialized state-of-the-art facilities across STEM, management, and arts.
Where is the campus located and how do I visit?
Our flagship campus is located in the heart of Bengaluru. You can schedule a guided campus tour by contacting our admissions office or booking a slot through our website's Admissions page.
Are the academic programs offered by Vyasa accredited globally?
Yes, our curriculum is globally benchmarked and our institutions hold accreditations from leading national and international educational boards, ensuring seamless transition for higher education abroad.

ACADEMICS & ENROLLMENT

Admission Criteria & Requirements

Details regarding qualification criteria, fee structures, student admissions, and application schedules.

What are the primary admission requirements and selection criteria?
We look for balanced profiles including consistent prior academic history, performance in our specialized cognitive assessment tests, and active engagement in co-curricular/sport activities. Individual requirements differ based on the selected grade levels.
How do I initiate the online application process?
You can initiate the process by clicking the "Apply Now" button at the top of our website. You will be prompted to create an applicant profile, upload required documents, and pay the application fee.
What is the fee structure and do you offer financial aid?
Our fee structure varies by grade and program. We do offer merit-based scholarships and need-based financial aid for qualifying students. Please refer to the Fee Structure section on the Admissions page for detailed breakdowns.
What specialized technological resources are available to students?
Students have access to smart classrooms, advanced robotics and AI labs, extensive digital libraries, and personalized learning tablets pre-loaded with interactive educational software.

RESIDENTIAL LIFE

Vyasa Hostels & Campus Ecosystem

Learn about our separate housing wings, nutritious meals, security networks, and medical response structures.

What safety measures are implemented for hostel residents?
Safety is our top priority. Our hostels feature 24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric access control, full-time resident wardens, and a strict no-unauthorized-visitor policy to ensure a secure environment.
How is medical assistance handled on campus?
We have a fully equipped on-campus infirmary staffed by qualified nurses 24/7, along with an on-call pediatrician. For emergencies, we have tie-ups with leading multi-specialty hospitals within a 5km radius and a dedicated campus ambulance.

Still Have Unanswered Questions?

Our admissions concierge is standing by to clarify any technical questions regarding admission schedules, fee segments, transport routes, or hostel amenities.
