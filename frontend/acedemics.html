ACADEMIC FRAMEWORK

A Seamless Continuum of CBSE Excellence

Our curriculum model values systematic discovery over simple retention. Designed to align with the CBSE National Board guidelines, we nurture deep competency in scientific, literary, and social parameters.

01

Primary School

GRADES 1 - 5

Foundational literacy, mathematical thinking, and experiential sciences following CBSE paradigms with active discovery methods.

03

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Inquiry as the Compass

At Vyasa, we believe that education is not the simple replication of validated data. Our philosophy posits each student as an independent investigator, equipped with analytical rigor and guided by high ethical values to shape a progressive global community.

"Education is not the filling of a pail, but the lighting of a fire."
