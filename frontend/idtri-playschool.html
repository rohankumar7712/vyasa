SCIENTIFIC PLAY

Designed Around Early Cognitive Milestones

IDTRI isn't just about fun. Our curriculum represents over a decade of research into early childhood development, cognitive scaffolding, and neurological development in ages 2-6.

PEDAGOGY BASE

The Scaffolding Method

We carefully structure physical play and building sessions so that children independently bridge logic gaps, enhancing spatial-temporal reasoning before formal reading begins.

NEUROLOGICAL

Sensory Integration

Our labs stimulate motor neuron paths through textured natural wood, dynamic water pathways, and safe tactile craft, improving emotional self-regulation.

SOCIAL DYNAMICS

Cooperative Autonomy

Designed group projects encourage self-direction while teaching healthy resource-sharing, verbal negotiations, and early leadership patterns.

CREATIVE SPACES

World-Class Innovation Labs for Toddlers

We build dedicated child-friendly zones that combine cutting-edge sensory interfaces with safe, tactile natural environments to foster unrestricted curiosity.

Sensory & Light Lab

A dynamic room designed to explore color spectrums, shadow play, and calming ambient tactile interfaces that nurture visual perception.

Maker Space & STEM Lab

Tactile mechanical blocks, safe pulleys, and gear systems for toddler mechanics.

Bio-Discovery Lab & Outdoor Play

Interacting with soil, seeds, and butterflies to instill early environmental stewardship.

CREATIVE OUTLETS

Tangible Wonders From Small Hands

Our children express their logical patterns through curated tactile projects. Rather than rote tests, we celebrate physical expressions of discovery.

NATURAL DYES

The Fingerprint Ecosystem

Children mapped campus ladybugs, flowers, and leaves using safe organic vegetable dyes to explore biodiversity.

STRUCTURAL PLAY

Tiny Architectural Arches

Using wooden cylinders and cardboard arches to discover force-bearing curves and balance configurations.

CLAY & CRAFT

Terracotta Nesting Cups

Hand-molding organic river clay into nested metric sizes, exploring physical dimensions and relative scales.
