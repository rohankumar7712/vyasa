About Vyasa International School

A distinguished K-12 learning institution committed to academic eminence, comprehensive global readiness, and grounding in true values. We nurture the standard-bearers of tomorrow.

OUR MILESTONES

Two Decades of Transformative Education

1998

The Foundation

VYASA Group established with a vision to provide quality education in Bangalore. First campus inaugurated.

2003

Campus Expansion

Second campus opened. Student enrollment crosses 2,000. New sports and arts facilities added.

2008

Academic Excellence

Consistently ranked among top institutions. Board exam results exceed 95% pass rate.

2012

Technology Integration

Smart classrooms, digital learning platforms, and computer labs established across all campuses.

2016

Research & Innovation

Launched student research programs, science olympiad teams, and innovation labs.

2020

International Collaborations

Partnerships with international schools. Virtual exchange programs initiated.

2024

New Horizons

AI-powered learning tools, sustainable campus initiatives, and expansion to new locations.

OUR VISION

"To be a globally recognized institution nurturing future-ready leaders grounded in Indian values."

We aspire to design a dynamic learning environment where innovation meets heritage, enabling students to step confidently onto the world stage while respecting their roots.

OUR MISSION

"To provide transformative education through innovation, inclusivity, and excellence."

We execute this by incorporating advanced technology systems, promoting active peer collaboration, practicing deep empathetic pastoral care, and fostering creative student initiative.

OUR CHRONOLOGY

Timeline of Milestones

Reflecting on twenty years of visionary K-12 learning, standard-setting board results, and community leadership.

2005

Foundation of Vyasa

Established with a humble vision to create a world-class educational ecosystem. Began our journey with our very first batch of grades 1-5.

2010

First Campus Expansion

Inaugurated our multi-purpose sports complex and state-of-the-art computer labs. Expanded admissions through Grade 10 under the CBSE curriculum.

2015

International Partnerships

Achieved global partnerships and introduced modern teaching frameworks. Recognized as a model school for conceptual clarity and global exchange programmes.

2020

Smart Campus & Advanced STEM Wing

Constructed our flagship Innovation Hub, including dedicated Robotics and Advanced Biotechnology labs to prepare students for the future.

2025

20th Anniversary Celebration

Celebrating two decades of excellence, consistency in Board Examination toppers, and a flourishing global alumni network leading industries globally.
