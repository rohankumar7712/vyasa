INSPIRING SPACES

Our World-Class Facilities

At VYASA, we believe that exceptional architecture and thoughtfully designed environments are critical catalysts for learning. Our campus blends state-of-the-art academic resources, sports arenas, and creative zones to nurture every student's absolute potential.

ACADEMIC FOUNDATIONS

Designed for Deep Focus & Collaboration

Where progressive pedagogy meets ergonomic, technology-driven physical environments designed to encourage active student participation.

Smart Classrooms

Our classrooms are designed as dynamic learning labs. Equipped with interactive digital smart-boards and high-definition multimedia projection tools, they enable our educators to turn complex theoretical concepts into engaging visual stories.

  • Interactive digital smart-boards in every room
  • Climate-controlled, well-ventilated spaces
  • Ergonomic seating arrangements facilitating collaborative layouts
  • Acoustically optimized design to minimize distraction

The Knowledge Oasis

With a monumental inventory of print resources and robust digital archives, the Vyasa Library is a modern sanctuary for intellectual exploration. Thoughtfully divided into quiet research zones and collaborative group discussion lounges.

"The library remains the intellectual heartbeat of our campus, encouraging students to journey from basic literacy to advanced, independent research."

— VYASA CHIEF LIBRARIAN
  • Over 50,000 reference titles, journals, and fiction works
  • High-speed digital library terminals for research and database access
  • Curated reading lounges with quiet, high-back ergonomic seating
  • Integrated academic counseling and research desk assistance
SCIENTIFIC INNOVATION

State-of-the-Art Labs & Maker Spaces

Empowering students to shift from theoretical models to hands-on testing, software engineering, and robotic design.

Advanced Science Labs

Workstations

Physic, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories equipped with premium instrumentation. Every workstation features safety valves, digital meters, and modern microscopes to enable precise empirical testing.

Next-Gen Computer Labs

Coding setup

Equipped with high-performance desktop terminals, modern IDE configurations, and fast internet access. Our digital labs are optimized for advanced machine learning, algorithmic study, and app design.

Robotics & AI Lab

Competition-ready

A state-of-the-art makerspace containing 3D printers, rapid-prototyping boards, drone-assembly stations, and sensory processing rigs. Serves as our engineering base for state & national robotics competitions.

PHYSICAL EDUCATION & HEALTH

Championship Sports Facilities

Promoting teamwork, physical resilience, and leadership through top-tier outdoor arenas and professional athletic complexes.

OUTDOOR ARENAS

Elite Sports Complex

Featuring an expansive professional grass football ground, pristine red clay cricket practice pitches, synthetic basketball courts, and a multi-lane Olympic athletic running track. All fields are supported by professional-grade illumination for evening training sessions.

AQUATICS

Olympic-Size Swimming Pool

An indoor, temp-regulated, 8-lane competitive pool overseen by certified lifeguards. Equipped with clean showers, separate locker facilities, and a dedicated pool deck for viewer seating during school tournaments.

INDOOR COURTS & FITNESS

Multi-Sport Indoor Stadium

Featuring elite maplewood flooring mapped for badminton, basketball, and table tennis. Includes a professional wellness center and gym staffed with trained physiotherapy assistants.

CAMPUS LIFE

Vibrant Community & Student Housing

Creating a warm, supportive, and hygienic space for cultural performances, wholesome dining, and safe residential housing.

The Grand Auditorium

1000+ Seats

Our majestic performing arts hall features state-of-the-art acoustic design, advanced digital mixing desks, theatrical lighting rigs, and seating to accommodate over 1000 visitors.

Hygienic Cafeteria

ISO Certified

A sprawling, bright dining hall serving nutritious, diet-managed vegetarian meals. Prepared in an ultra-hygienic stainless steel kitchen under the observation of food nutritionists.

Vyasa Hostels

Separate wings

Offering premium separate housing complexes for boys and girls. Supervised 24/7 by resident wardens, featuring common recreation rooms, and a nurturing home-like study environment.

SAFETY & OPERATIONS

Rigorous Care & Protection Services

Operating behind the scenes to keep our students safe, healthy, and seamlessly connected across Bengaluru.

24/7 Medical Centre

Ambulance

Our campus features a fully-stocked clinical medical desk supervised by resident nursing staff. Includes diagnostic beds, basic monitoring setups, and a dedicated 24/7 standby ambulance.

GPS-Tracked Transport

AC Fleet

A premium fleet of temperature-controlled school buses covering major routes in Bengaluru. Each bus is fitted with GPS trackers, speed limiters, and is staffed with school transit marshals.

Complete Campus Security

Biometric

Secured with comprehensive CCTV networks, biometric attendance tracking at entrance corridors, and highly trained security guards managing strictly logged visitor entry gates.
