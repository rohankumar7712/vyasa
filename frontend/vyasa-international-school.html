Frequently Answered Questions

Find immediate answers regarding curriculum standards, safety policies, and admissions timelines.

What curriculum frameworks are available? +

Vyasa primarily offers the CBSE curriculum, enriched with global STEM methodologies and active international project options for select grades.

What is the teacher-to-student ratio? +

To ensure personalized guidance, we maintain a strict 1:15 ratio in Pre-Primary levels and 1:24 in higher cohorts.

Do you offer school transport and safe tracking? +

Yes, our private fleet of buses covers all major sectors in Bangalore, complete with real-time GPS tracking and certified female attendants.

Is scholarship support available? +

Yes, merit-based and sports-specific financial aid packages are reviewed annually by the Vyasa Trust.
