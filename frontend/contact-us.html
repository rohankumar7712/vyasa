Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding admissions, fees, transport services, and academic guidelines.

What are the admission requirements for VIS and PU College? +

Admission requires a completed application form, proof of age (birth certificate), previous academic transcripts, transfer certificates, and a successful basic assessment/interaction with the school curriculum advisors.

What are the fee payment options and schedule? +

Fees are structured transparently and can be paid annually or in quarterly installments. We accept net banking, online gateway payments, demand drafts, and major credit/debit cards.

How do I reach the campus and is transport available? +

The campus is conveniently located near Vidyaranyapura, North Bangalore. We operate a fleet of safe, GPS-enabled school buses covering prominent residential zones in Bangalore.

What facilities are available on-campus? +

We offer state-of-the-art science labs, modern IT labs, smart classrooms, a digital library, dynamic sports fields (basketball, athletics), and creative rooms for performing arts.

Is hostel accommodation available for students? +

We primarily cater to day scholars. However, we provide assistance with verified premium housing partners for our PU College students coming from other states.

What is the step-by-step admission process? +

The process flows seamlessly: 1) Online/Offline Enquiry, 2) Campus Tour & Interaction, 3) Application & Documentation, 4) Fee Payment & Final Confirmation of seat.
