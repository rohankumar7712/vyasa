OUR FRAMEWORK

Assessment & Grading System

How we measure progress and success.

EVALUATION STRATEGY

Holistic Progress & Continuous Growth

We track every student's intellectual, emotional, and social development across their journey to ensure well-rounded achievement.

Our Core Philosophies

Our evaluation frameworks extend far beyond traditional testing. We believe that true learning requires active participation, creative problem solving, and consistent effort.

  • Regular Formative Assessments instead of high-stakes exams
  • Emphasis on collaborative projects and oral presentations
  • Development of portfolios to showcase long-term progress
  • Continuous feedback loops involving students, parents, and teachers

ASSESSMENT METHODS

Multi-Dimensional Evaluation Framework

Our varied assessment methods ensure every learning style is supported and evaluated accurately.

Formative Assessments

Daily Check-ins

Short quizzes, interactive polls, and exit tickets used to gauge immediate understanding and pivot teaching strategies.

Summative Examinations

Term End

Comprehensive tests designed to evaluate the retention and application of knowledge over a complete academic term.

Project & Tech Portfolios

Year Round

Students compile major coding projects, lab reports, and essays to demonstrate mastery of complex concepts over time.

Peer & Self Evaluation

Collaborative

Promoting self-reflection and constructive critique, students learn to evaluate their own work and the work of their peers.

ACADEMIC SUPPORT

Support Ecosystem & Remediations

We ensure no child is left behind. Our tailored support programs assist students who need extra guidance.

Remedial Care & PTMA

Vyasa is built on the foundation of inclusive learning. For students facing academic hurdles, we construct personalized remediation strategies.

"Our goal is not just to grade students, but to guide them. If a student struggles, it is an opportunity for us to adapt our teaching methods, not a failure of the child."

DR. MEERA SUNDARAM, HEAD OF ACADEMICS
  • After-school remedial classes with specialized tutors
  • Psychological support and academic stress counseling
  • Detailed action plans developed jointly during PTMA meetings
  • Peer-mentoring networks allowing students to learn from peers
