Frequently Asked Questions

Quick, helpful briefings regarding pre-university academics, board registration frameworks, and campus facilities.

What competitive exams coaching is integrated?
+
Vyasa offers fully integrated classroom preparation for JEE Main/Advanced, NEET, and KCET alongside state board curricula, saving student travel time.
Are separate boys and girls hostel facilities provided?
+
Yes, our modern, completely secure residential blocks are segregated, offering clean single/double rooms, premium catering, and round-the-clock wardens.
What are the college hours for Science and Commerce streams?
+
Science stream integrated timings generally run from 8:30 AM to 3:30 PM (including mock tests). Commerce batches run from 8:30 AM to 1:30 PM.
Does the college offer transport tracking facilities?
+
Absolutely. Our fleet of security-vetted buses covers all prime sectors in Bangalore, complete with GPS live tracking and physical student trackers.
