Senior Mathematics Teacher

Full-Time On-site ₹50,000 - ₹70,000 / month
Posted by Admin Director Closes: Sept 30, 2026

About the Role

Vyasa International School is seeking an enthusiastic and experienced Senior Mathematics Teacher to join our dedicated faculty. The ideal candidate will have a passion for mathematics and a proven ability to engage and inspire students in grades 9 through 12.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and deliver comprehensive lesson plans aligned with the curriculum (CBSE/ICSE/IGCSE).
  • Utilize innovative teaching methods, including educational technology, to enhance learning.
  • Assess student progress, provide constructive feedback, and prepare students for board examinations.
  • Participate in departmental meetings, parent-teacher conferences, and extracurricular activities.
  • Mentor junior teachers and contribute to curriculum development.

Requirements

  • Master's degree in Mathematics or a related field.
  • B.Ed. or equivalent teaching certification.
  • Minimum of 5 years of teaching experience at the senior secondary level.
  • Excellent communication, interpersonal, and classroom management skills.

Benefits

We offer a competitive salary, professional development opportunities, health insurance, and a supportive, collaborative work environment.
